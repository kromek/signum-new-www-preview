About

Signum IT is a software engineering company. The company operates as P.P.H.U. Signum-IT Przemysław Kromołowski, based in Szarlejka near Wręczyca Wielka, Poland.

Scope of activity

Signum IT delivers integration and automation, custom software, AI agents and skills, and training in the use of AI. The details are on the Solutions page.

Experience

The company builds on more than 20 years of software engineering experience.

Specialisations

Signum IT specialises in C/C++ and Python, and has expertise in embedded systems (embedded and firmware), industrial automation and protocols, and applications of AI. The full list of technologies is on the Expertise page.

Way of working

Cooperation follows four steps: consultation, analysis, delivery and support. Signum IT starts by understanding the client's problem and only then chooses the tools.

Contact

The contact person is Przemysław Kromołowski. Contact details are on the Contact page.
