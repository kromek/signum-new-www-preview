Custom software

Applications built around a client's specific process, such as a mobile warehouse app and production tracking.

The problem

Off-the-shelf software assumes its own way of working. When your process looks different, the software may need to be adapted.

What we do

Signum IT builds production applications tailored to the client's specific process, instead of bending the process to fit a program.

Example scopes of work

  • mobile warehouse app: an application supporting work in the warehouse,
  • production tracking: up-to-date insight into what is happening on the production floor,
  • other production applications built around a described process.

Technologies

The choice of technology depends on the process and the client's environment. The areas Signum IT works in are described on the Expertise page.
