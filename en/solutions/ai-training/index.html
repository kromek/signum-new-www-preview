AI training

Training in using AI in desktop applications that carry out tasks like an employee.

The problem

AI is often used only as a chat in the browser, although desktop applications exist that carry out tasks like an employee.

What we do

Signum IT trains people to use AI in desktop applications (Claude, ChatGPT) that act like an employee carrying out tasks, not like a chat in the browser.

Example scope

  • working with AI in the Claude and ChatGPT desktop applications,
  • handing tasks over to AI, which carries them out on its own,
  • the scope and programme are agreed after a conversation about your company's needs.

Technologies

Claude, ChatGPT.
